These files are served same-origin (never from a third-party CDN) and
are lazy-loaded only when the stream studio starts a camera with the
Pro background engine. The .wasm binary matching the selected
execution provider (WebGPU -> jsep build, otherwise the plain build)
is the only one a browser downloads.
