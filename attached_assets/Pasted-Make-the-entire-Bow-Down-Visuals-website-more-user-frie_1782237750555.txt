Make the entire Bow Down Visuals website more user-friendly, simple, and easy to understand.

Do not add Stripe.
Do not add new APIs.
Do not remove existing core features.
Do not break OpenAI generation, Runway generation, Artist Vault, Music Studio, saved projects, or user login.

Goal:
Make the site feel easy for a beginner music creator.

The user should always understand:
1. What this page does
2. What to do next
3. What each button does
4. What is coming soon vs what works now

GLOBAL UX CHANGES

1. Simplify language across the site.

Replace technical words with simple creator-friendly words.

Examples:
- “Generate Runway Clip” → “Create Video Clip”
- “Scene Cards” → “Video Scenes”
- “Music Studio” → “Music Mixer”
- “AI Auto Mix & Master” → “AI Mix”
- “Manual DAW Mode” → “Manual Mixer”
- “Export Final Video” → “Download Finished Video”
- “Project Folder” → “My Projects”
- “Artist Vault” → “Artist Profiles”
- “Transcribe Lyrics” → “Get Lyrics From Song”
- “Analyze Song Sections” → “Find Hook & Verses”

Keep advanced terms in small helper text if needed.

2. Add helper text under every major page title.

Each page should explain itself in one sentence.

Examples:

Dashboard:
Choose what you want to create today.

Make a Song:
Create lyrics, hooks, beat ideas, and an AI music prompt.

Make a Music Video:
Turn your song or lyrics into video scenes and AI video clips.

Make Song + Video:
Create a full song idea and a video plan in one workflow.

Promo Clips:
Create TikTok, Reels, and Shorts ideas for your song.

Artist Profiles:
Save your artist look, style, colors, and brand so your visuals stay consistent.

Music Mixer:
Upload vocals, beats, and stems to preview or export a mix.

Video Editor:
Edit clips, captions, music, effects, and export your finished video.

3. Add a simple “What happens next?” box on every workflow page.

Example:
Step 1: Fill in your song info
Step 2: Generate your result
Step 3: Save or edit it
Step 4: Download or open the editor

4. Add tooltips or small info text for confusing features.

Examples:

Runway clips:
AI video clips are silent previews. Your song audio is added during final export.

Artist Profiles:
Use this to keep your artist’s look and style consistent.

Credits:
Credits are used when you generate AI results.

Music Mixer:
Upload separate audio files like vocals, beat, drums, or ad-libs.

5. Make buttons clearer.

Use button labels like:
- Start Full Workflow
- Create Song
- Create Music Video Plan
- Create Video Clip
- Save This Project
- Open Editor
- Download Text
- Download PDF
- Choose Artist
- Change Artist
- Upload Song
- Upload Stems
- Preview Mix
- Download Audio
- Download Finished Video

Avoid vague buttons like:
- Submit
- Run
- Process
- Execute
- Generate Data

6. Add empty states.

If there are no saved projects, show:
No projects yet. Start by making a song or music video.

If there are no artist profiles, show:
No artist profiles yet. Create one to keep your visuals consistent.

If there are no uploaded clips, show:
No video clips yet. Create a video plan first, then create clips from your scenes.

If there are no audio stems, show:
No stems uploaded yet. Upload vocals, beats, or a full song to start mixing.

7. Add beginner mode.

Create a simple toggle or setting:
Beginner Mode: On / Off

When Beginner Mode is on:
- hide advanced settings by default
- show simple explanations
- show fewer options at once
- use step-by-step cards
- keep advanced controls collapsed

Beginner Mode should be on by default for new users.

8. Clean up navigation.

Main navigation should stay simple:
- Dashboard
- Make Song + Video
- Make Music Video
- Promo Clips
- Artist Profiles
- Pricing

Top-right should include:
- Credits
- My Projects
- User menu

Do not overload the toolbar.

9. Dashboard should guide the user.

Dashboard should have a section:
What do you want to do?

Cards:
- I want to make a full song + video
- I already have a song and need a video
- I want promo clips for TikTok/Reels
- I want to create or choose my artist profile

Each card should explain what happens when clicked.

10. Improve the Start Creating flow.

Start Creating should do:
- if logged out: send to sign in
- after sign in: choose artist or continue without artist
- then go to Dashboard

Make the Choose Artist page simple:
Title:
Choose Your Artist

Subtitle:
Pick an artist profile to keep your songs, videos, colors, and style consistent.

Buttons:
- Use This Artist
- Create New Artist
- Continue Without Artist

11. Improve page layouts.

Use:
- step-by-step wizards
- progress bars
- collapsible advanced sections
- preview panels
- simple cards
- clear headings
- short explanations
- fewer things visible at once

12. Add “Coming Soon” labels.

Clearly label features that are not fully working yet:
- Advanced mastering
- Full pro effects rendering
- Team accounts
- Real AI vocal generation if not connected yet
- Advanced beat making if not connected yet

Do not make coming-soon features look like broken buttons.

14. Add small onboarding checklist for new users.

Show on Dashboard:
Getting Started

Checklist:
1. Create or choose an artist profile
2. Make a song or upload your song
3. Create a music video plan
4. Create video clips
5. Open the editor
6. Save or download your project

15. Keep the style premium but easier.

Keep:
- dark cinematic look
- purple glow
- silver/white text
- luxury creator studio feel

But make it:
- less cluttered
- larger buttons
- clearer sections
- more spacing
- simpler wording
- easier mobile layout

Final goal:
A first-time creator should understand the site in under 60 seconds.
