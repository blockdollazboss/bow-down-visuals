Fix Master Player sync drift before export.

Problem:
The master player is not consistently in sync.
Sometimes the audio/video is synced, sometimes it is not.
That means the preview player itself is drifting, so export cannot be trusted yet.

Do not change:
- payments
- credits
- captions
- effects
- overlays
- watermark
- Sync.so
- existing clips
- existing project audio
- existing video-only export

Only fix:
- master player audio/video sync
- master player clock/timeline control
- preview playback stability
- locked timeline playback

Core issue:
The master player must not let video clips and audio run on separate independent clocks.

Main rule:
Use the song audio element as the single master clock.

Requirements:

1. The project audio must be the master clock.

Every frame of video should be selected based on:

currentAudioTime

Do not let each video clip free-run independently without checking the audio clock.

2. Build a deterministic timeline playback system.

For each clip:
- timelineStart
- timelineEnd
- duration
- videoUrl
- trimStart
- trimEnd
- lipSyncOffset if needed

At any audio time, calculate:
- active clip
- local video time = currentAudioTime - clip.timelineStart + trimStart
- corrected lip sync video time if lipSyncOffset exists

3. On play:
- start audio
- start active video
- seek active video to correct local time
- keep checking sync

4. Add drift correction.

Every 250ms check:
- expected video local time
- actual video currentTime
- drift = actual - expected

If drift is greater than 0.08s:
- seek video back to expected local time

If drift is small:
- leave it alone to avoid jitter

5. On scene/clip change:
- pause old video
- load next video
- seek next video to correct local time
- play next video only after it can play
- keep audio as the master clock

6. Add visible Master Sync Debug panel:

Master Player Sync:
- audio current time
- active scene
- active clip index
- expected video time
- actual video time
- drift amount
- correction applied: yes/no
- lip sync offset
- audio is master clock: yes/no
- video free-running: yes/no

7. Add button:
Reset Master Player Sync

When clicked:
- pause playback
- seek audio to 0:00
- seek active video to 0:00
- clear drift state
- restart from locked timeline

8. Add button:
Lock Master Timeline

This should save the exact timeline used by the master player:
- clip order
- clip starts
- clip ends
- clip durations
- trims
- lip sync offsets

9. Master player must use the locked timeline after it is created.

Show:
Master Player Source:
- using locked timeline: yes/no
- timeline id
- clip count
- audio starts at 0:00 yes/no

10. Export Engine V2 should not run until master player is stable.

Add status:
Master Player Stable:
- audio is master clock: yes/no
- drift under 0.08s: yes/no
- no missing clips: yes/no
- locked timeline ready: yes/no

11. Do not rely on React render timing for sync.
Do not rely on setTimeout to guess clip timing.
Do not let video determine the master timeline.
The audio clock controls everything.

Final goal:
The master player must play the same way every time. Audio starts at 0:00, video clips follow the audio clock, and drift is corrected live. Once master player sync is stable, Export Engine V2 can use the same locked timeline for final export.
