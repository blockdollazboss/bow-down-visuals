Rebuild Bow Down Visuals around a Suno + CapCut all-in-one workflow.

Bow Down Visuals should not just be a video generator.
It should be a music creation and music video editing platform.

Main product direction:
Bow Down Visuals = Suno + CapCut in one website.

Core workflow:

1. Create or upload song
- write lyrics
- generate song
- upload song
- analyze song structure
- detect intro, hook, verse, bridge, outro
- show song waveform/timeline

2. Create music video scenes
- generate scene ideas from lyrics
- create clips for each song section
- keep artist character consistent
- assign clips to timeline sections

3. CapCut-style editor
- audio timeline is the main clock
- video clips snap to song sections
- drag clips
- trim clips
- split clips
- replace clips
- add transitions
- add captions
- add effects
- add overlays
- add logo watermark
- preview everything in sync

4. Export engine
- export must use the same locked timeline as the editor
- audio starts at 0:00
- clips follow the audio clock
- captions follow lyrics timing
- final export matches preview exactly

Important:
Do not keep patching the old broken export system forever.
Build a clean editor timeline engine where the song audio is the source of truth, like CapCut.

Technical rule:
The song/audio timeline controls everything:
- video clip start times
- clip durations
- captions
- effects
- transitions
- lip sync scenes
- export timing

Add a new section called:
Bow Down Studio Editor

This should feel like a simple CapCut editor for music videos.

Start with MVP:
- song upload
- waveform timeline
- 12 video clips on timeline
- master player synced to audio clock
- clip trim controls
- locked timeline JSON
- export video + audio from the locked timeline

Do not add advanced captions/effects until the basic editor timeline and export are synced perfectly.

Final goal:
Bow Down Visuals should become a full AI music video studio: make the song, make the video, edit it, sync it, and export it all in one place.
