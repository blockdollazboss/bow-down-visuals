The fix: on a phone, tapping any section button on the side rails now opens that section as a bottom sheet over the canvas (shown open on Text / Captions below). All 29 sections work this way — Media, Templates, Audio, Timeline, Effects, Branding, Promo Clips, Export and the rest — using the exact same panels the desktop editor shows. Tap the ✕, tap outside the sheet, or press back and you are back on the canvas; the play / transport bar at the bottom is untouched and stays where it was. On a bigger screen nothing changes — the desktop editor looks exactly as before.
