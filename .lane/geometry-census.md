## Parked with evidence

### Item 90 — backdrop LOD (fog-ring skyline)

The facet's backdrop (16 towers, 8 domes, colonnade at full geometry) does not exist in this tree. The shipped backdrop is 3 unlit wing masses (2 boxes each, 12 tris), one instanced draw of emissive windows, one instanced draw of 9 garden masses (Sphere(1,12,8) = 168 tris each), a 40-tri ground skirt disc and a 96-tri moat ring segment — about 10 draws and ~1.7k triangles in total.

There is nothing left to LOD: the geometry is already minimal boxes and instanced draws, all of it MeshBasicMaterial silhouettes inside fog. A THREE.LOD swap could only remove a handful of 12-triangle boxes at the cost of a pop at the swap distance. Stays parked — measured, not assumed.

### Item 91 — district LOD pass (as LOD — braziers, monument reliefs)

Braziers: 6 placements, ~132 tris of solid parts each — converted by instancing instead (see kit census). The Kingdom Board monument is ONE hero object at the plaza's centre: 2 relief heights × 4 faces of gold bands + 4 relief panels + tiers, drum and crown — every part a different size (the bands follow the stele's taper), ~45 meshes / ~600 tris total, nothing repeated identically. The chandelier (the third named target) converts by instancing above.

LOD swaps geometry at distance; the monument is the plaza's centrepiece — the walker is always near it, and its parts are unique sizes with no far form to swap to that isn't a different-looking object. Braziers and chandelier got their draw savings from instancing, which changes no pixel at any distance. LOD-as-LOD stays parked with these numbers as the reason.

### Item 95 — figure draw budget (pooled / impostor far form)

Per-figure draws today: capsule figure = 4 (torso Capsule(0.3,0.78,4,12) = 216 tris, head Sphere(0.21,18,14) = 468 tris, visor, floor blob); portrait/default figures = 4–5 (portrait plane + frame + blob + nameplate sprite). Facet-E's 'canvas textures uploaded eagerly for every figure including off-screen' is not in this tree: no initTexture/upload call exists anywhere in the world code — three.js uploads a CanvasTexture on its first rendered frame, and figures in rested rooms are not rendered.

Every figure is unique — its own presence tint, portrait texture, nameplate, mouth and per-frame pose — so there is no identical kit to instance, and the pooled/impostor route replaces a person with a flat billboard at distance: a visible change to how people look, which the premium bar forbids as a side effect. The honest budget lever for figures stays the presence cap and the room cull that already exist. Stays parked — measured, not assumed.
