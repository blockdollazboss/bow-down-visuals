# CapCut Feature Research (Oct 2026)

## Timeline Editing
- Multi-track timeline (video, audio, text, stickers, effects, overlays)
- Unlimited video layers, trim, split/blade, ripple delete/trim
- Magnetic timeline + snapping, picture-in-picture
- Drag-and-drop, frame-by-frame trim, timeline zoom
- Clip duplicate, multi-level undo/redo, reverse playback, freeze frame
- Group/link clips, batch "Apply to All", AI scene detection, proxy editing, markers

## Effects & Filters
- 100+ preset effects (Trending, Lens, Light, Retro, Glitch, Particles, etc.)
- Body effects (motion trails, outlines), glitch, distortion, light leaks, VHS/retro
- 3D Zoom, generative AI effects (natural language → effect)
- Effect intensity slider, overlays, animated stickers, split screen
- Masking, blend modes, chroma key, AI Auto Cutout (background remover)
- Camera shake, vignette, cinematic bars, video-in-text, sky replacement, face blur

## Transitions
- 50+ transitions (Dissolve, Wipe, Slide, Zoom, etc.)
- One-tap apply, adjustable properties, match-cut to beats, motion-based

## Text & Captions
- Auto captions (AI speech-to-text, ~98% accuracy, multilingual)
- Caption styles (Trendy, Bold, Neon), word-by-word/karaoke, keyword highlights
- Manual editing, text animations, custom fonts/uploads, text-to-speech, SRT export

## Audio
- Multi-track audio, royalty-free music library, SFX library
- Voiceover recording, AI text-to-speech (20+ languages), voice changer/cloning
- Vocal isolation, noise reduction, audio ducking, EQ, loudness normalization
- Fades, volume keyframes, beat sync, extract/detach audio, reverb

## Speed & Retiming
- Speed curve (Bezier) with presets (Montage, Hero, Bullet, etc.)
- Uniform 0.1x–100x, optical-flow slow-mo, reverse, freeze frame

## Animation
- Keyframes (position, scale, rotation, opacity, etc.), easing
- Motion tracking, Auto Reframe (16:9 → 9:16)

## AI Features
- Auto Cutout, upscaler, voice enhancer, auto-captions, TTS
- Script-to-video, text-to-video (Seedance), AI auto-edit, silence removal
- Generative effects, color enhance, stabilization, voice cloning

## Color Grading
- Brightness/contrast/saturation/temp, HSL, curves, color wheels
- LUT support (.cube/.3DL), filter library, scopes (waveform/vectorscope)

## Export
- Up to 4K 60fps, 24–60fps, MP4/MOV, bitrate control, Smart HDR
- Platform presets, watermark-free, SRT export

## Templates
- Thousands of templates, one-tap use, AI templates, create/share/monetize
